Privacy Policy

Crescendo · effective October 27, 2026 (updated with safety features)

Crescendo (“the app”) is developed by DaKlok (“we”). This policy explains what data the app handles. In short: the app has no ads, analytics or tracking, and we never sell data. Without an account, everything stays on your device or in your own Google Drive. If you create an optional Crescendo account, the data needed for sync and friends is stored in Google Firebase, as described below.

Data stored on your device

The app stores the PDF files you import, their thumbnails, your annotations, folders and app settings in the app’s private storage on your device. This data is not sent to us or any third party.

Camera

If you turn on hands-free page turning, the app uses the camera to detect head movements and facial gestures. The image is analysed in real time on your device using Google ML Kit (on-device face detection). Camera images are never saved, recorded or transmitted.

Microphone

The microphone is used only when you use the tuner or record yourself practising. The tuner analyses sound in real time on your device and stores nothing. Recordings are saved only in the app on your device (and in your own backup, if you make one).

Free sheet music catalog

When you open the catalog, the app downloads a list of pieces from the app’s website (daklok.github.io). When you add a piece, the PDF is downloaded directly from the Mutopia Project (mutopiaproject.org). No personal data is sent, only standard web requests.

Google account and Google Drive

Backup to Google Drive is optional. If you connect your Google account, the app requests:

  • Your email address: only to show which account is connected.
  • Access to the app’s own hidden folder on your Google Drive (scope drive.appdata: to upload, list, download and delete Crescendo backups. The app cannot see or access any other files on your Drive.

If you are signed in to a Crescendo account and connect Google Drive, the app also keeps your sheet music PDFs and recordings in the same hidden folder, so that your other devices can download them. A random device identifier and the device model name are stored with them so that the app can tell which device made the latest changes.

Backups contain your imported PDFs, annotations, folders and settings and, with a Crescendo account, a copy of your chats with friends. They are transferred directly between your device and Google Drive over an encrypted connection and are never sent to our servers (we do not operate any). You can disconnect your account in the app’s settings at any time and delete the backup data in Google Drive under Settings → Manage apps.

Crescendo’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not used for advertising, sold, shared with third parties or used to train AI models.

Crescendo account (optional)

You can create an account with Google or with an email address and password (Google Firebase Authentication). The account stores your email address, name and profile photo link. It is used to keep your library the same on all your devices and to practise with friends. The data below is stored in Google Cloud Firestore and is transferred over an encrypted connection:

  • Sync: your list of pieces (titles, composers and similar details), folders, setlists, annotations, bookmarks, practice log, practice spots and some settings. Only you can read this data. Sheet music PDFs and recordings are not stored here (see Google Drive above).
  • Friends: your profile (name, photo, friend code) and practice statistics (streak, minutes today, this week and in total, the last 7 days and, if you allow it, the title of the piece you practised last) are visible to the friends you accept.
  • Sending music: music you send to a friend (the PDF and the parts you choose, such as annotations or recordings) is stored for 30 days after sending, so your friends can accept it and download it again if they remove it from their library. Music sent through a shared setlist is deleted as soon as everyone has it.
  • Shared music: annotations of a piece you share are visible to and editable by its members. While the music is open, the other members see which page you are on and where you last wrote. If you allow it, they also see how much you practised that piece and which practice spots you have finished.
  • Shared setlists: the title, the list of pieces and the names of the members.
  • Chat: messages you write to a friend or in a group, and what the chat shows besides them (music sent there, edits in shared music, changes in the group), are visible to the members of that chat. A group also stores its name, its members and who manages it. The chat remembers how far each member has read, so a friend can see that you have seen a message. You can delete your own messages; devices of the other members that already received a deleted message keep its text hidden, only so that it can still be reported. A chat with a friend is deleted when the friendship is removed, a group when its manager deletes it or its last member leaves. The app also keeps a copy of your chats on your device, so you can read them offline and keep them when a chat ends; this copy is part of your backup (see Google Drive above) and you can delete it in the app. A clap for a friend's practice is a message in your chat with them; claps from older versions of the app are kept in the friend's account for at most 30 days.
  • Safety: the list of people you blocked (their account IDs, names and photo links) is stored in your account and only you can see it; it is used to stop their friend requests and hide their content. If you report something, the report contains what you reported (the message, music, group or person), the names and account IDs of the people involved, the messages of that chat around it (at most 100), your optional note, your account ID and name and the app version. Only we can read reports; we use them only to handle the report and keep them for at most one year, or longer only if the law requires it (for example when we must pass them to the police). The person you report is not told who reported them.

The app checks pages of sheet music you send and receive for nudity with a small model built into the app (based on the open source nsfwjs project), and it can hide offensive words in chat. Both checks run only on your device; no pages or messages are sent anywhere for them.

You can sign out at any time. Delete account in the app’s settings deletes your account and its data in Firestore (profile, friendships, chats with friends, your messages in groups, your list of blocked people, sync data and music you sent that was not accepted yet) and removes you from groups, shared music and setlists. Annotations you wrote in shared music stay with the other members. See the Firebase privacy information for how Google processes this data.

Digital piano (MIDI and Bluetooth)

If you connect a digital piano by USB cable or Bluetooth MIDI, the app receives the notes and pedal presses you play to record your playing, show the keys in the visualizer and turn pages with the pedal. This data is processed on your device only. Bluetooth permissions are used only to find and connect to your piano.

Notifications and Do Not Disturb

If you turn on the practice reminder, the app shows a local notification once a day. It is created on your device; nothing is sent anywhere. With a Crescendo account, the app checks your account about every 15 minutes for new messages, music, friend requests and setlist invitations and shows a notification on your device; you can turn this off in Friends, or mute a single chat. In stage mode the app can turn on Do Not Disturb, only if you allow it.

Purchases (Crescendo Pro)

Crescendo Pro is sold through Google Play. Payments are handled entirely by Google; we never receive your payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether your Google account owns Crescendo Pro or an active subscription, and remembers the answer on your device. See the Google Privacy Policy for how Google processes purchases.

Analytics and advertising

The app contains no ads, analytics or tracking.

Data deletion

Uninstalling the app removes all data from your device. Your Crescendo account and its data can be deleted with Delete account in the app. Backups stored in your Google Drive can be deleted from the app or from Google Drive. Backup ZIP files you export are stored where you choose and are under your control.

Children

Crescendo can be used by music students of any age. Without an account the app collects no personal information. The account, friends and chat are meant for people who know each other (friends can only be added with their personal code); if you are under 16, use them only with the consent of your parent or guardian. If you believe a child uses an account without that consent, contact us and we will delete it. See also the community rules and our child safety standards.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
