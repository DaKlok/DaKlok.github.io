Contents

Crescendo (“the app”) is developed by Samuel Káčer (“we”), who is responsible for your data in the app. This policy explains in plain words what data the app handles, why, where it is stored and how you can delete it. In short: the app has no ads, analytics or tracking, and we never sell data. Your Crescendo account and the data needed for sync, friends and the cloud are stored in Google Firebase, as described below.

Your Crescendo account

To use Crescendo you sign in with a Crescendo account, created with Google or with an email address and password (Google Firebase Authentication). Your library, annotations and practice log belong to your account, so they are never lost or mixed up between people and you have them on all your devices. The account stores your email address, name and profile photo link and a random account ID.

Data stored on your device

The app stores the PDF files you import or scan, their thumbnails, your annotations, folders, recordings and app settings in the app’s private storage on your device. Scanning a document uses Google’s document scanner (ML Kit in Google Play services); the scanned pages are returned to the app and stay on your device. Text recognition used to find titles and measures in your music also runs on your device.

Sync

The following data is stored in Google Cloud Firestore and is transferred over an encrypted connection. Only you can read it:

  • your list of pieces (titles, composers and similar details), folders, setlists, annotations, bookmarks, practice log, practice spots and some settings;
  • a random device identifier and the device model name, so the app can tell which device made the latest changes.

Sheet music and recordings in the cloud (Pro)

With Crescendo Pro, the files of your pieces – sheet music PDFs, reference and own recordings, MIDI and learned page turns for turn by sound – and daily restore points of your library are stored in Google Cloud Storage, up to 1 GB. Only your account can access these files. A file you delete stays in the cloud for 30 more days, because restore points may still need it. A server function keeps count of how much space your account uses.

Without Pro, sheet music and recordings stay only on your device. You can always make a backup to a file; it is stored where you choose and is under your control.

Friends, sharing and chat

  • Friends: your profile (name, photo, friend code) and practice statistics (streak, minutes today, this week and in total, the last 7 days and, if you allow it, the title of the piece you practised last) are visible to the friends you accept. Friends can only be added with their personal code or QR code; scanning a QR code uses Google’s code scanner and the camera image is not shared with the app.
  • Sending music: music you send to a friend (the PDF and the parts you choose, such as annotations or recordings) is stored for 30 days after sending, so your friends can accept it and download it again. Music sent through a shared setlist is deleted as soon as everyone has it. A daily sending limit is counted per account and deleted after a few days.
  • Shared music: annotations of a piece you share are visible to and editable by its members. While the music is open, the other members see which page you are on and where you last wrote. If you allow it, they also see how much you practised that piece and which practice spots you have finished.
  • Shared setlists: the title, the list of pieces and the names of the members.
  • Chat: messages you write to a friend or in a group, and what the chat shows besides them (music sent there, edits in shared music, changes in the group), are visible to the members of that chat. A group also stores its name, its members and who manages it. The chat remembers how far each member has read, so a friend can see that you have seen a message. You can delete your own messages; devices of the other members that already received a deleted message keep its text hidden, only so that it can still be reported. A chat with a friend is deleted when the friendship is removed, a group when its manager deletes it or its last member leaves. The app keeps a copy of your chats on your device, so you can read them offline. A clap for a friend’s practice is a message in your chat with them.

Safety

The list of people you blocked (their account IDs, names and photo links) is stored in your account and only you can see it; it is used to stop their friend requests and hide their content. If you report something, the report contains what you reported (the message, music, group or person), the names and account IDs of the people involved, the messages of that chat around it (at most 100), your optional note, your account ID and name and the app version. Only we can read reports; we use them only to handle the report and keep them for at most one year, or longer only if the law requires it (for example when we must pass them to the police). The person you report is not told who reported them.

The app checks pages of sheet music you send and receive for nudity with a small model built into the app (based on the open source nsfwjs project), and it can hide offensive words in chat. Both checks run only on your device; no pages or messages are sent anywhere for them.

Camera

If you turn on hands-free page turning, the app uses the front camera to detect head movements and facial gestures. The image is analysed in real time on your device using Google ML Kit (on-device face detection). Camera images are never saved, recorded or transmitted.

Microphone

The microphone is used only when you use the tuner, turn by sound, or record yourself practising. Sound for the tuner and turn by sound is analysed in real time on your device and is not stored. Recordings are saved in the app on your device and, with Pro, in your cloud storage (see above).

Digital piano (MIDI and Bluetooth)

If you connect a digital piano by USB cable or Bluetooth MIDI, the app receives the notes and pedal presses you play to record your playing, show the keys in the visualizer, write music and turn pages with the pedal. This data is processed on your device only. Bluetooth permissions are used only to find and connect to your piano.

Free sheet music catalog and sounds

When you open the catalog, the app downloads a list of pieces from this website (daklok.github.io). When you add a piece, its files are downloaded directly from the Mutopia Project (mutopiaproject.org). Instrument sounds for playback are downloaded from this website or public mirrors (cdn.jsdelivr.net, raw.githubusercontent.com). These are standard web requests; no personal data is sent with them.

Notifications and Do Not Disturb

If you turn on the practice reminder, the app shows a local notification once a day; it is created on your device. The app checks your account about every 15 minutes for new messages, music, friend requests and setlist invitations and shows a notification on your device; you can turn this off in Friends, or mute a single chat. In stage mode the app can turn on Do Not Disturb, only if you allow it.

Purchases (Crescendo Pro)

Crescendo Pro is a subscription sold through Google Play. Payments are handled entirely by Google; we never receive your payment details. To unlock the cloud for your account, the app sends the purchase token to our server function, which checks it with Google Play and stores the product, the purchase token, its state and expiry date with your account ID. Google Play also notifies the server when a subscription is renewed, cancelled or refunded. See the Google Privacy Policy for how Google processes purchases.

Security

All data the app sends is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Access to your data in Firebase is protected by security rules, so other people can only see what is described above. Firebase App Check with Google Play Integrity makes sure that only the genuine app from Google Play can reach our Firebase services.

Analytics and advertising

The app contains no ads, analytics or tracking, and data is never sold or used for advertising or to train AI models.

Data deletion and retention

Delete account in the app (Settings → Account and backup) permanently deletes your account and its data in the cloud: profile, friendships, chats with friends, your messages in groups, your list of blocked people, sync data, sheet music, recordings and restore points in the cloud, and music you sent that was not accepted yet. It also removes you from groups, shared music and setlists. Annotations you wrote in shared music stay with the other members. Reports are kept as described under Safety. Uninstalling the app removes all data from your device.

You can also ask us to delete your account without the app – see Delete your account.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it or to delete it, and you can object to its processing. Write to the address below. If you live in the European Union, you can also complain to your data protection authority (in Slovakia, the Office for Personal Data Protection). See the Firebase privacy information for how Google processes data on our behalf.

Children

Crescendo is intended for users aged 13 and over. Friends can only be added with their personal code, and chat is meant for people who know each other. If you are under 16, use the account, friends and chat only with the consent of your parent or guardian. If you believe a child uses an account without that consent, contact us and we will delete it. See also the community rules and our child safety standards.
