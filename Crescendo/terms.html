Not allowed:

Your tools: press and hold a message to report it, report music, a group or a person, and block anyone. A blocked person can no longer write to you, send you music or send you a friend request, and their messages in shared groups are hidden. The app also hides offensive words (you can turn this off) and checks pages of sheet music you send and receive for nudity. Both run on your device; nothing is uploaded for these checks. If someone is in danger, call 112 or your local emergency number.

What we do: we read every report. Content that breaks these rules can be removed, and accounts that break them can be suspended or deleted, without warning in serious cases. If you are under 16, use chat only with the consent of your parent or guardian.
