Use of the app

The app is for reading, annotating and writing sheet music, practising and sharing music with people you know. You need a Crescendo account to use it. You are responsible for having the right to use the PDF files and recordings you import.

Your content

All files, annotations and backups remain yours. We do not have access to them, except for content someone reports to us (see Community rules).

Community rules

With a Crescendo account you can chat with friends you add with their code and in groups (for example a teacher with students or a band), and send them sheet music. Before you write your first message or send music, the app asks you to agree to these rules. They apply to messages, names, group and setlist titles, sheet music, recordings and annotations you share.

Not allowed:

  • Insults, bullying, harassment, threats and hate against anyone, for example because of their origin, religion, sex, sexual orientation or disability.
  • Nudity, sexual content and violence, also inside sheet music, pictures or annotations.
  • Anything that sexualises or endangers children, and any contact with children for sexual purposes.
  • Encouraging self-harm, drugs or other dangerous behaviour.
  • Sheet music or recordings you do not have the right to share.
  • Spam, scams, pretending to be someone else and sharing other people’s personal data.

Your tools: press and hold a message to report it, report music, a group or a person, and block anyone. A blocked person can no longer write to you, send you music or send you a friend request, and their messages in shared groups are hidden. The app also hides offensive words (you can turn this off) and checks pages of sheet music you send and receive for nudity. Both run on your device; nothing is uploaded for these checks. If someone is in danger, call 112 or your local emergency number.

What we do: we read every report. Content that breaks these rules can be removed, and accounts that break them can be suspended or deleted, without warning in serious cases. If you are under 16, use chat only with the consent of your parent or guardian.

Child safety

We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation. Any such content or behaviour is removed, the accounts involved are deleted, and we report it to the police and to the relevant authorities as required by law.

Report it in the app (reason A child is at risk) or write to us at the address below; for urgent danger call 112.

Crescendo Pro

Crescendo is free to use. Crescendo Pro is an optional monthly or yearly subscription sold through Google Play. It renews automatically until you cancel it in Google Play; you keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for. Payments and refunds are handled by Google Play under its terms. The cloud storage included in Pro is limited to 1 GB per account. Features of Pro are described in the app before you subscribe.

Crescendo keeps evolving, and so does Pro. New features often arrive in Pro first and may later become part of the free version for everyone (for example, turn by sound). We may also add, change or improve Pro features over time. The current list of Pro features is always shown in the app; if a change affects your subscription significantly, you can cancel it in Google Play at any time.
