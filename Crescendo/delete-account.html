Delete your account

You can permanently delete your Crescendo account and all its data in the cloud at any time – right in the app, or by email if you no longer have the app.

Contents

In the app

The fastest way. Your account is deleted right away.

  1. Open Crescendo and sign in with the account you want to delete.
  2. Go to Settings → Account and backup.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm with Delete permanently.

Without the app

Write to us from the email address you use to sign in to Crescendo (for a Google account, the address of your Google account). Use the subject Delete my Crescendo account. We delete the account within 30 days and send you a confirmation.

Email a deletion request

If you write from a different address, we may ask you to confirm that the account is yours.

What is deleted and what stays

Deleted permanently

  • Your account: email, name and profile photo
  • Synced library, annotations, practice log and settings
  • Sheet music, recordings and restore points in the cloud
  • Friendships, chats with friends and your messages in groups
  • Your block list and music you sent that was not accepted yet

What stays

  • The library on your device – uninstall the app to remove it
  • Annotations you wrote in shared music stay with the other members
  • Reports involving you, for at most one year (longer only if the law requires it)
  • The record of your Pro purchase in Google Play (managed by Google)

Deleting your account does not cancel Crescendo Pro. Cancel it in Google Play: Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Crescendo.

Delete only some data

You can keep your account and delete only some data:

  • Messages – press and hold a message and choose Delete.
  • Pieces and recordings – delete them in your library; they leave the cloud within 30 days.
  • Friends – removing a friend also deletes your chat with them.

For anything else, write to support.crescendo.com@gmail.com. More in the Privacy Policy.
