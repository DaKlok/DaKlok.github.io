Help us launch Crescendo

Before Crescendo is released to everyone, Google Play needs at least 12 testers to try it for 14 days. Joining takes two minutes, and you get the app before anyone else – for free.

Join the testers group

Open the group and tap Join group. Use the same Google account you use in Google Play on your phone.

Open the group

groups.google.com/g/crescendo-app-testers

Become a tester

On the testing page, tap Become a tester. This page only works once you are in the group from step 1.

Become a tester

play.google.com/apps/testing/com.daklok.crescendo

Install Crescendo

Open Crescendo on Google Play and install it. Then please keep it installed for at least 14 days and play something in it now and then.

Open on Google Play

play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.daklok.crescendo

Google Play says the app isn’t available

After joining the group and tapping Become a tester, it can take a few minutes for the app to appear. Make sure Google Play uses the same account as the group – if you have several accounts on your phone, switch it with your profile picture in Google Play.

Why 14 days?

Google requires new apps to be tested by at least 12 people for 14 days in a row before they can be published. If you uninstall the app or leave the test earlier, it doesn’t count.

Does it cost anything?

No. Crescendo is free to use and you don’t need to buy anything to test it.

How do I send feedback?

Write to the group or to support.crescendo.com@gmail.com. You can also leave private feedback right on Google Play.

How do I leave the test?

Open the page from step 2 again and tap Leave the program. You keep the app, and it later updates to the public version.

Thank you!

Every tester helps Crescendo reach everyone.
