Questions

Pro questions

Is there a free trial?

Not at the moment. The free version has no limit on pieces and no time limit, so you can try the app at your own pace.

How do I cancel?

In Google Play: profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Crescendo → Cancel. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.

Can I get a refund?

Payments and refunds are handled by Google Play under its rules. In the EU you have 14 days to withdraw from a subscription. Request a refund in Google Play, or write to us.

Does Pro work on all my devices?

Yes. Pro belongs to your Crescendo account, so you have it on every device where you sign in – even if Google Play there uses a different Google account.

What happens to my music if Pro ends?

Nothing is lost. Your library, annotations and practice log keep syncing for free and the music on your devices stays. New files just won’t upload to the cloud anymore and Pro features lock again.
