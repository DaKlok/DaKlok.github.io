Getting started

How do I add my sheet music?

Tap the + button on the home screen. You can import a PDF from your device or cloud storage, scan paper sheet music with the camera, or download a piece from the free score catalog.

Which devices does Crescendo run on?

On Android phones, tablets and foldables with Android 8.0 or newer. Sheet music reads best on a tablet.

How do I turn pages hands-free?

In the reader, open the ⋮ menu → Head turning settings. You can turn pages by turning or tilting your head (the camera image is processed only on your device), with the pedal of a connected digital piano, or with Pro also by the sound of your playing.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. Reading, annotating and practising work without internet. Changes sync when you’re back online. You need internet to sign in, for the score catalog and for friends.

Which languages is the app in?

English and Slovak. Change it in the app’s settings, or on Android 13 and newer in system Settings → Apps → Crescendo → Language.

Account & sync

Why do I need an account?

Your library, annotations and practice log belong to your account, so they’re never lost and you have them on every device. The account is free – sign in with Google or with email.

What syncs for free?

Your list of pieces, folders, annotations, bookmarks, practice spots, setlists and practice log. The sheet music files and recordings themselves move between devices through the cloud with Pro.

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password? and we’ll email you a link to set a new one.

How do I back up my library?

In Settings → Account and backup. A backup to a file is free and contains everything, sheet music and recordings included. With Pro, a restore point is also made in the cloud every day.

How do I delete my account?

In Settings → Account and backup → Delete account, or write to us. Everything about what gets deleted is on the Delete account page.

Pro & billing

What’s included in Pro?

Every annotation tool, the piano visualizer, turn by sound, setlists and stage mode, PDF export and 1 GB of cloud. See the comparison on the Pricing page.

How do I cancel my subscription?

In Google Play: profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Crescendo → Cancel. You keep Pro until the end of the paid period. Deleting your account or uninstalling the app does not cancel it.

I bought Pro but don’t have it

Check that you’re signed in with the same Crescendo account you bought Pro with. Then tap Restore purchases in the Pro window. If that doesn’t help within a few minutes, write to us with your account email.

Can I get a refund?

Payments and refunds are handled by Google Play under its rules. In the EU you have 14 days to withdraw from a subscription. Ask in Google Play or write to us.

Friends & chat

How do I add a friend?

Open Friends and tap Add a friend. Enter their friend code, scan their QR code, or send them your invite link. Nobody can add you without your approval.

Who can see my practice?

Only friends you accepted: they see your streak and practice minutes. They see the title of the piece you practised last only if you allow it.

How do I report or block someone?

Press and hold a message and choose Report, or use Report or Block in the person’s profile or chat menu. A blocked person can no longer message you or send you music. We read every report.

Privacy & data

Does the camera record me?

No. For head turning the camera image is processed in real time only on your device and is never saved or sent anywhere.

Who can see my sheet music?

Only you – unless you send them to a friend or a group. There are no ads or tracking and we never sell data. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Where is my data stored?

On your device and in Google Firebase (Google Cloud), over an encrypted connection. Security rules protect access, so only you can see your private data.
